# 📖 SWE-Jobs User Guide

Welcome to **SWE-Jobs**, your ultimate companion for finding the perfect software engineering role. This guide will help you master the bot's features and the web dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

To begin, search for [@YourBotUsername] on Telegram and send the `/start` command.

### 1. The Multi-Topic Channel
The main channel is divided into **14 specialized topics**. Instead of one giant feed, you can focus only on what matters to you:
- **Frontend / Backend / Mobile**
- **AI/ML & Data / DevOps**
- **Egypt Jobs / Saudi Jobs** (Location-specific)
- **Internships** (For fresh grads and students)

---

## 🔔 Personalized Alerts (/subscribe)

Don't want to check the channel every hour? Use the `/subscribe` command to set up **Personalized DM Alerts**.

1.  **Select Topics:** Choose which categories you want to track.
2.  **Filter by Seniority:** Only want "Senior" or "Lead" roles? Just toggle them.
3.  **Keywords:** Add specific keywords (e.g., "React", "Rust", "Part-time").
4.  **Salary Threshold:** Set a minimum yearly salary (e.g., $50,000).

The bot will send you a private message the moment a matching job is found!

---

## 🔍 Searching & Saving

### Search the Archive (/search)
Our database stores thousands of jobs. Find exactly what you need with:
`/search python remote` or `/search react senior egypt`

### Save for Later
Every job post has a 💾 **Save** button. 
- Click it to bookmark a job.
- View all your bookmarks anytime with the `/saved` command.

### Track Your Applications
Tap the ✅ **Applied** button on a job post to keep a history of your job hunt. Check your progress and daily streak with the `/applied` and `/streak` commands.

---

## 🚫 Managing Your Feed (/blacklist)

Tired of seeing jobs from a specific recruiter or company? 
Use `/blacklist add company AcmeCorp` to never see them again in your DMs.

---

## 📊 Insights & Analytics

### Bot Stats (/stats)
Get a quick snapshot of the job market:
- How many jobs were posted today vs. this week.
- Which sources (LinkedIn, Remotive, etc.) are the most active.

### Salary Insights (/salary)
Curious about market rates? Type `/salary backend` to see the average, lowest, and highest salaries for that role based on the last 30 days of data.

### Web Dashboard
For the full experience, visit our **Web Dashboard**. It features:
- **Skill Trends:** See which technologies are becoming more popular.
- **Detailed Charts:** Visualize the job market across different countries and sources.
