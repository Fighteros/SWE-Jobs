# SWE-Jobs Telegram Guide

Find the bot in Telegram and send `/start` to see its welcome message and commands.

## Job alerts

Use `/subscribe` to create a personalized alert. Choose the topics and filters offered by the bot, such as seniority, location, sources, keywords, and salary. Use `/mysubs` to review alerts, `/unsubscribe` to remove them, and `/blacklist` to filter out companies or keywords.

## Search and applications

- `/search <keywords>` searches recent jobs, for example `/search python remote`.
- Tap **Save** on a job to bookmark it; use `/saved` to view bookmarks.
- Tap **Applied** to record an application; use `/applied` to review applications.
- Use `/streak` to view your application streak.

## Insights and help

- `/stats` shows job and source activity.
- `/top` shows popular jobs.
- `/salary <role>` shows salary insights for a role.
- `/status` reports source health.
- `/contact` sends feedback to the maintainers.
- `/help` lists available commands.

Admin-only commands include `/messages` and `/broadcast`.
